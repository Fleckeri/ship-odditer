              Nerd Stuff

              • Drop shares come from player-logged missions (mixed research levels) and are assumed not to depend on capacity.
              • Per launch: average copies of the item, in the shown rarity, from one launch (its share of logged drops × capacity). P(≥1): the chance one launch returns at least one; each of the capacity items is drawn independently, so it's 1 − (1 − share)capacity. 50% in: launches for a 50% chance of at least one. Set the odds with the Odds slider.
              • Rare, epic, and legendary versions have stone slots. Stones, fragments, and ingredients only come in common.
              • Tier summaries: "All in one launch" is the chance one launch has every listed item. "At 50% odds: all in …" is the launches needed to have seen them all at your odds; the rarest item dominates it. "… target in …" does the same for the target's item, or says when it doesn't drop there.
              • The strip shows P(≥1) at each star level; the outlined cell is the selected level.
              • Target uses only drops logged with that target set. The game doesn't publish how targeting works, and the boost varies widely, so nothing is estimated. Only the Quintillion Chicken and later can target. The count beside each target in the list is how many items were logged with it for this ship, length, and star level; "no data at N★" means none yet. Stone fragments are hidden: targeting them is almost never worthwhile (a poor return, and lower odds of tier 2–4 stones), so consume artifacts for fragments instead.
              • Capacity = (base + per-star bump × stars) × (1 + 5% × Zero-G level), × 2 or × 3 during a capacity event, rounded down. FTL cuts mission time 1% per level, from the Quintillion Chicken up; it changes days, not drops. Quality runs from the mission's minimum to its maximum plus the per-star bump.
              • Star progress counts launch points (1 per short, 1.4 per standard, 1.8 per extended launch) against the game's star thresholds. "From scratch" starts at 0★; "From N★" starts when the ship reaches N★.
              • Days assume three slots launching together and relaunching as they land. Fueling usually makes you slower.
              • Faded, underlined values (≈ in tooltips) rest on fewer than 5 sightings and are rough. Thin data for a whole mission is flagged above the tables.
